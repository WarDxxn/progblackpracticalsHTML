What is League Of Legends?

League Of Legends is a online MOBA (Multiplayer Online Battle Arena) game consisting of two teams of 5 players. The main 5v5 competitive game mode is played on a map called Summoner's Rift. The three most popular game modes are as follows:

Game Mode # Players Allowed Description
Ranked Solo/Duo 1 or 2 Players League's premier competitive 5v5 gamemode played on Summoners Rift
Ranked Flex 1,2,3 or 5 players A Ranked queue generally for larger groups of players including 5 stacks
ARAM 1 to 5 players Played on Howling Abyss, a single lane map, ARAM stands for all random all mid.
Each team consists of the following roles:
  • Top
  • Mid
  • Bottom
  • Support
  • Jungle
